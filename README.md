## `// About Me`

I'm a Computer Science (AIML) student who genuinely enjoys the craft of building for the web — the kind of person who'll spend an extra hour getting a hover state or a loading transition just right. Day to day that means HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, split between shipping projects and working through Data Structures & Algorithms, because I want the "make it work" instinct and the "make it work *well*" instinct to grow together.

Machine learning is where my coursework and my curiosity overlap the most. I like that AIML forces me to think in systems and probabilities, not just pixels and props — and long-term, that's the direction I'm building toward: a full-stack developer who's equally at home shipping a polished interface and reasoning about the model behind it, eventually working at the intersection of the two in interactive, AI-assisted, immersive web experiences.

- 🎨 Drawn to interfaces that feel considered — spacing, motion, and the small details most people only notice when they're missing
- ⚛️ Building with React today, reaching further into **Three.js** and WebGL for more spatial, immersive interfaces
- 🤖 Studying AI & Machine Learning alongside web development, actively looking for where the two meet
- 🧩 A problem-solver at heart — I'd rather understand *why* something broke than just patch it
- 🧱 Care about clean, maintainable code, and building things that are responsive and accessible by default
- 🌱 Learning in public — reading open-source code, picking up new tools, slowly contributing back
- 🐍 Creator of **SnakeVerse**, one of a few real, shipped projects I keep iterating on
- 🤝 Enjoy collaborating — building alone teaches you code, building with others teaches you how software actually gets made
- 🎯 Goal: grow into a **Full Stack Developer & AI Engineer**, working on interactive, intelligent web experiences
